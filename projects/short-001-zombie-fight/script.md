# Short 001 — Zombie Fight Test

## Goal
Create an 8–10 second high-energy Minecraft fight animation test.

This is the quality gate for the reusable Vellous animation pipeline. It should test character motion, impact timing, camera choreography, debris, lighting, motion blur, and sound synchronization.

## Sequence

### 0:00–0:02 — Wall Crash
Steve crashes backward through a wooden village wall.

- Wall breaks around his body rather than disappearing at once.
- Splinters and block fragments travel toward and past camera.
- Camera tracks backward with Steve.
- Steve rotates slightly in the air and lands with visible momentum.
- Start immediately with action; no intro card.

### 0:02–0:04 — Recovery
Steve lands, rolls, slides, plants one hand, and rapidly recovers.

Three zombies rush through the damaged wall behind him.

- Keep Steve moving continuously.
- Low camera rises with Steve during recovery.
- Dust follows his slide.
- Zombies should have different stride timing so the group does not look duplicated.

### 0:04–0:06 — Dodge
Steve draws his sword as the first zombie attacks.

- Zombie winds up before swinging.
- Steve ducks underneath the attack.
- Weapon passes close to camera.
- Camera reframes during the dodge instead of hard-cutting too early.

### 0:06–0:08 — Counter Combo
Steve counterattacks.

- First hit redirects the zombie.
- Second movement uses Steve's torso and hips, not only his arm.
- Zombie is launched sideways with readable momentum.
- Use a short camera recoil exactly on contact.

### 0:08–0:10 — Continuation Hook
Camera orbits Steve as he transitions into the next attack.

- Remaining zombies close in.
- End on an active mid-fight moment rather than a static pose.
- Frame the final moment so it can continue seamlessly into the full Short later.
