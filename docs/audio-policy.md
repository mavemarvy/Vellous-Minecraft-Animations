# Audio Policy

## Default Policy
- Prefer original music and original sound design.
- Do not rely on copyrighted trending songs for master exports.
- Use Minecraft in-game audio only where its use is permitted and appropriate.
- Keep an internal provenance record for created and licensed audio assets.
- Every video project should include an `audio-plan.json` manifest.
- Do not scrape or redistribute audio from YouTube, TikTok, or other platforms.

## Audio Layers
A finished cinematic mix may contain:
- ambience
- footsteps and movement
- character or mob sounds
- weapon movement
- impacts
- debris and destruction
- transitions and risers
- sub-bass accents
- original music
- dialogue or narration
- intentional silence

## Synchronization
Major sounds should be aligned to animation frames. For example:
- anticipation
- whoosh start
- contact frame
- impact transient
- bass hit
- camera recoil
- debris
- recovery
